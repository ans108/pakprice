INFINIX INBOOK Air XL442 in Pakistan

Presenting to you an ultra-light laptop for everyday use, the INFINIX INBOOK Air XL442. Features a 12th Gen Intel Core processor, 256 GB SSD storage, 8 GB DDR4 memory, a 14-inch WUXGA display, and Windows 11 Home.

Boasting a simple but refined grey finish, it delivers optimum performance with day-to-day tasks. Whether you're a student, freelancer, or a professional, it meets your needs with style, power, speed, and efficiency.

12th Gen Intel Core i3 Processor

This laptop offers a hexacore processor, the 12th Generation Intel Core i3-1215U. Based on Alder Lake architecture, it features 8 threads, 6 cores, and 10 MB cache. The mix of 4 efficient cores and 2 performance cores submits unwavering support for multitaskers.

The CPU has a base frequency of 1.2 GHz that reaches up to 4.4 GHz, good enough for handling both mid-level and high-end tasks flawlessly. The 10 MB cache memory boosts data access, which is a big plus for achieving higher productivity.

It ensures low power consumption for extended hours of run time, keeps the speed sustainable, and gives you a seamless multitasking experience.

Integrated Graphics

The notebook has Intel integrated graphics built into the 12th Gen Intel Core i3 chip. It handles visual tasks effortlessly and allows light gaming. It supports 2 displays, 4K videos, and handles Adobe apps quite well. For daily tasks, it provides quality visuals without any lag.

14-Inch WUXGA Display

This machine has an exceptional 14-inch Liquid Crystal Display for enhanced clarity, and at the same time, energy efficiency. Furthermore, the display is Widescreen Ultra Extended Graphics Array (WUXGA) that delivers a screen resolution of 1920×1200p. The 16:10 Aspect Ratio gets you a taller screen, making it apt for reading, writing, and editing. 

Moreover, the 300 nits of peak brightness is good for using the machine in a bright environment. 

8 GB DDR4 Memory

It offers 8 GB DDR4 memory for a seamless multitasking experience. Whether you switch between web browsers, documents, or light editing work, it operates in a smooth manner, with improved responsiveness and enhanced load time. It runs at a speed of 3200 MHz and directly contributes to the processing outcomes.

256 GB SSD Storage

This laptop offers a robust and spacious 256 GB SSD storage. It carries PCIe technology to achieve faster data transfer, and NVMe for faster read/write speed. Moreover, the M.2 form factor goes well with ultra-thin and lightweight laptops and machines, having limited space inside. 

Backlit Chiclet Keyboard

It features a backlit Chiclet keyboard that illuminates the keys from beneath while typing. Great for typing while working in dim light. Indeed, it is a great support for busy users with its enhanced ergonomic and comfortable usage.

720p HD Camera and Powerful Battery

It also offers a 720p HD camera, so the communication with your teammates doesn’t stop while you are on the go. Plus, its powerful battery doesn’t run out on you.

Sleek, Smart, and Portable Design

It features a sleek and smart design that looks irresistibly elegant in a grey finish. Weighing no more than 1.24 kg (2.7 lbs), it is super lightweight. Moreover, the aero-grade aluminum material boosts its durability and gets you the best value for money.

Other Specs

Also offers Wi-Fi 6 802.11AX, Bluetooth 5.2, audio jack, USB-C port, two USB 3.2 Gen 1 ports, an HDMI, Windows 11 Home, and a 1-year warranty.
